CAST AND BLOCKING. One singer on the podium. 66 dancers in three concentric rings around the podium (10 inner, 20 middle, 36 outer), all facing the singer in the center; the rings turn in alternating directions and the dancers crouch on the off-beat every 2 seconds. Dancers wear royal blue hooded robes with yellow trim, hood up and a blue cloth over nose and mouth, so the singer is the only visible face. Twelve mirror monoliths stand in a circle around the stage; they reflect only sky, sand and blurred blue robes, never a clear face or a second copy of the singer.

Deep dark red suit, white shirt, small royal blue butterfly bow tie with a gold pattern (never a long necktie), long crimson red overcoat moving in the wind. He wears classic round black sunglasses with a thin gold metal frame the whole time and never takes them off; the lenses stay dark black with no red tint or glow. Natural proportions, confident and joyful, never distorted.

STYLE AND LIGHTING. Photoreal, 35mm film look. The sky is the only light: soft, even, diffuse dusk light with gentle mist and haze, low contrast, muted warm sand and a slightly cool sky. Soft even light on the singer's face. No harsh sun, no lens flares, no strong orange or red cast.
