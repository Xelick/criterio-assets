Music video, 30 seconds, one continuous generation with hard cuts. A singer performs on a podium in a desert ritual concert at dusk, surrounded by 66 dancers and 12 mirror monoliths.

THE ASSETS. The reference video is a grey 3D previz and the master reference for camera, cuts, shot sizes, positions and counts: follow it strictly, and where this text and the video disagree, the video wins. CAST AND BLOCKING. One singer on the podium. 66 dancers in three concentric rings around the podium (10 inner, 20 middle, 36 outer); the rings turn in alternating directions and the dancers crouch on the off-beat every 2 seconds. Dancers wear royal blue hooded robes (#2A2AE6) with yellow trim (#FFD400), hood up and a blue cloth over nose and mouth, so the singer is the only visible face. Twelve mirror monoliths stand in a circle around the stage.

SINGER. Same face, long wavy dark hair, thin mustache and sparse short chin beard as the character sheet. Deep dark red suit, white shirt, small royal blue butterfly bow tie with a gold pattern (never a long necktie), long crimson red overcoat moving in the wind. Natural clear eyes, natural proportions, confident and joyful, never distorted.

STYLE AND LIGHTING. Photoreal, 35mm film look. The sky is the only light: soft, even, diffuse dusk light with gentle mist and haze, low contrast, muted warm sand and a slightly cool sky. Soft even light on the singer's face. No harsh sun, no lens flares, no strong orange or red cast.

CAMERA. Strictly as the reference video, second by second: same moves, same shot sizes, cuts exactly at 8.4 s, 16.8 s and 23.6 s, no cuts added or removed. Deep focus, 180 degree shutter, natural motion blur.

ACTION TIMING. 0 to 8.4 s: high top-down crane over the stage, descending and tilting to an aerial view of the rings and the mirror circle; the singer is a small red figure in the center. 8.4 to 16.8 s: low dolly at ground level moving forward through the dancers toward the podium, dancers passing close to the lens. 16.8 to 23.6 s: push-in from a medium shot to a chest-up shot of the singer at the microphone, never closer than chest-up; at 22.6 s he sings "¡Criterio!" with energy and a raised fist. 23.6 to 30 s: the beat drops; the camera orbits the stage at crowd level, the singer in the middle distance, dancers moving with more energy.
